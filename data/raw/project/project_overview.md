# Southampton Opportunity Engine

The Southampton Opportunity Engine is an AI-powered urban intelligence and business opportunity platform. It supports Southampton City Council, planners, entrepreneurs, investors, and residents by turning fragmented local data into evidence-based insights.

## Business ecosystem intelligence

The platform analyses industry trends, business births and deaths, commercial clusters, demographic characteristics, and neighbourhood differences. Its outputs include sector trends, commercial hotspots, and neighbourhood comparisons.

## Business and service gap detection

The platform investigates possible gaps in childcare, grocery stores, healthcare, independent retail, co-working space, professional services, and community facilities. It must distinguish community need from commercial viability because a socially valuable service is not necessarily profitable.

## Scenario simulation

A planner can compare alternative uses of a vacant commercial property, such as a childcare centre, independent cafe, or co-working space. The comparison considers accessibility, existing competition, demand indicators, service coverage, and alignment with planning priorities. The platform supports investigation and does not guarantee business success.

## Responsible AI

Recommendations must be transparent, supported by evidence, and explicit about uncertainty. The system should not present incomplete data as proof of unmet demand or guaranteed profitability. Final decisions remain with the council and relevant stakeholders.
